Privacy Policy

Mostafa Rclone Backup is a personal backup application operated by its owner.

Data Access

The application requests read-only access to the owner's Google Drive through Google OAuth 2.0.

Data Usage and Storage

Google Drive files are downloaded to a local computer controlled by the account owner for backup purposes.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell or share Google Drive file contents with third parties.

Data Retention

Backup files remain on the owner's local computer according to the configured backup retention policy.

Access Revocation

The owner can revoke the application's access through Google Account security settings.
